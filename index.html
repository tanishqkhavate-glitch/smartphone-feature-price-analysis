06 / Product recommendation

A balanced specification for the ₹18,999 position

The recommendation combines observed segment patterns with practical product judgement.
Recommended spec sheet

A capable everyday 5G smartphone

₹18,999

Position the device near the upper end of the ₹15K–₹20K band, with memory and connectivity aligned to common higher-rated configurations.

  • 8 GB RAM
  • 128 GB internal storage
  • 5G connectivity
  • 5,000 mAh or larger battery
  • Recent Snapdragon / Dimensity SoC
  • 50–99 MP camera with validated sensor quality
Before final launch

Commercial validation still required

  • Confirm bill of materials, margins and channel costs.
  • Benchmark exact chipset generation and sustained performance.
  • Validate camera sensor, OIS and image processing.
  • Check software support, warranty and competitor launch pricing.
  • Test customer demand and willingness to pay; this dataset does not measure either.
07 / Method and limitations

What is included, and what should be treated carefully

Transparent data treatment is part of the result, not a footnote.

Dataset and scope

Source: Kaggle Smartphone Models Dataset. Original dataset: 980 rows and 25 columns. Analysis table: 973 rows after seven unusual model records were set aside for manual verification. Target segment: models priced below ₹20,000.

All price bands use inclusive lower bounds and exclusive upper bounds.

Cleaning decisions

  • No exact duplicate rows or duplicate brand/model combinations were found.
  • Missing processor brand and operating system values were labelled “Unknown”.
  • Fast-charging and expandable-storage values were set to zero only when the availability flag was explicitly zero.
  • Other unknown numeric values were left missing rather than guessed.

Important limitations

  • 101 rating values (10.3%) are missing; the rating scale's meaning is not independently verified.
  • Several comparisons have small samples, especially 256 GB storage and 2.5+ GHz processors.
  • Observed relationships are not causal and may reflect brand, age or other confounding factors.
  • Regression uses one train-test split and should be validated further.
View the seven records excluded for manual verification

Tesla Pi Phone · Apple iPhone 14 Mini · Apple iPhone 15 Ultra · Samsung Galaxy Note 30 Ultra 5G · Nokia N73 5G · Nokia X60 5G · Nokia X60 Pro 5G. These records were flagged for verification, not conclusively labelled fake.

View the interpretation note

Average ratings are reported only for records with a non-missing rating. Unless explicitly labelled “rated models”, sample counts represent the records returned by the relevant segment query. Comparisons should be read as descriptive signals within this dataset, not as causal estimates or proof of consumer preference.
